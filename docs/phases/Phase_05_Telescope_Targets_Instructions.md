# Phase 05: Space Mission Targets, Cleanroom Hubble Facility & Educational Systems Engineering

## 1. Overview
Phase 05 expands the game with multi-target telescope mission selection, educational complexity toggling for students (Age 13+) and systems engineers, authentic NASA research terminals, and an overhauled 3D Cleanroom Facility with realistic aerospace ground support platforms.

## 2. Key Components

### 2.1 Multi-Target Telescope Selection
- Three authentic NASA flagship space observatories:
  1. Hubble Space Telescope (HST / STS-31): Primary 2.4-meter optical/UV survey telescope in 593 km LEO orbit.
  2. James Webb Space Telescope (JWST): 6.5-meter segmented beryllium gold infrared observatory at Sun-Earth L2 Lagrange Point.
  3. Chandra X-Ray Observatory: High-resolution grazing incidence mirror telescope in highly elliptical Earth orbit (HEO).

### 2.2 Educational Complexity Mode (Age 13+ vs Engineering)
- Dynamic complexity toggle in mission briefings and research terminals.
- High School / Introductory Level: Conceptual analogies, visual schematics, orbital overview, and foundational physics.
- Professional Engineering Level: Precise NASA standards (NASA-STD-5020, NASA-STD-8739), thermal budgets, structural torque margins, link budget calculations, and GMAT orbital mechanics.

### 2.3 Official NASA Research Terminal Citations
- Terminal research stations inside the 3D facility integrated with:
  - NASA GMAT R2026a (General Mission Analysis Tool): High-precision orbital propagation.
  - NASA Concurrent Design Facility & Mission Design Lab (MDL): Goddard Space Flight Center integrated mission design methodologies.
  - NASA Systems Engineering Handbook (SP-6105 / SP-2016-6105-REV2): Phase A to Phase F project lifecycle.
  - NASA CubeSat 101: Small satellite integration standards and verification guidelines.

### 2.4 Professional Aerospace Ground Support Equipment (GSE) Stand
- Replacement of legacy platform with authentic Goddard Cleanroom Work Platform:
  - Structural extruded aluminum perimeter frame.
  - High-friction ESD diamond-tread rubber composite decking.
  - Four precision hydraulic self-leveling corner jacks.
  - Non-skid safety approach ramps.
  - Telescoping vertical stanchions with dual yellow perimeter fall-arrest safety chains.
  - Realistic working eye-height alignment with player camera.
